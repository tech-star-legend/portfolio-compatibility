Who we are. Com SR Infotech is a software development company that partners with small businesses, startups, and institutions to design, build, and maintain the digital systems they rely on every day — from customer-facing websites to the internal software that keeps operations running.

Our story. Com SR Infotech was started with a simple observation: most small and mid-sized organizations are underserved by software vendors who either overcharge for generic templates or overpromise on enterprise-scale solutions they don't need. We started this company to sit in between — genuinely custom work, built by developers who take the time to understand the business first.

Looking ahead. As we grow, our goal stays the same: fewer clients, better work, longer relationships. We'd rather be the team a business calls for the next ten years than a vendor used once.

Our Mission

Practical software, honestly delivered

To give businesses of every size access to well-engineered digital tools — websites, software, and automation — without the inflated cost or complexity of enterprise consulting.

Our Vision

The dependable tech partner

To become the long-term technology partner that small businesses, schools, and startups in our region trust for every stage of their digital growth.
